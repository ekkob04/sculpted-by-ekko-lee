Meet Your Coach

Hi, I'm Ekko —
and I built Sculpted for you.

Ekko grew up as a lifelong equestrian on a small island off Vancouver Island, where dedicated training, discipline, and a deep appreciation for movement were built from a young age.

Now based in downtown Vancouver, Ekko specializes in strength training tailored specifically for the female body. Her coaching blends lower body development and hypertrophy with a balanced, holistic approach to long-term health, helping clients build confidence, manage issues like arthritis and back pain, and achieve sustainable results.
